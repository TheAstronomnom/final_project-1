My Dream Trip to Japan

From December 2 through December 18, 2024, my wife and I took the trip of a lifetime through Japan. We started in Kanazawa, then made our way through Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, and finally Tokyo. here are some of the highlights from each stop along the way.

Tip: Click any photo to view it full size.

Kanazawa

Our first stop. Kanazawa is known for its preserved samurai districts, historic gardens with stunning fall colors, and incredible station architecture.

Places we visited:

  • Nagamachi Samurai District
  • Nishi Chaya Shiryokan Museum
  • Kenrokuen Garden
  • Higashi Chaya District
  • Omicho Market
  • Nomura Samurai Residence
  • Kanazawa Castle Park
  • Kanazawa Station
  • Ishikawa Prefectural Library
  • Shirakawa-go
  • Takayama

Kyoto

Kyoto was a huge highlight. The Golden Temple was breathtaking, the Bamboo Forest was peaceful, and the Nintendo Museum was a dream come true for us.

Places we visited:

  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Temple)
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Forest
  • Nijo-jo Castle
  • Nintendo Museum

Osaka & Nara

Osaka was full of energy, great food, and Super Nintendo World. The Capcom and Godzilla stores were a blast.

Places we visited:

  • Osaka Aquarium
  • Port of Osaka
  • Capcom Store
  • Godzilla Store
  • Universal Studios Japan
  • Super Nintendo World
  • Nara Deer Park

Tokyo

We saved Tokyo for last. Asakusa was magical at night, we met legendary wood block printer David Bull at his shop. Here's a video I took walking through Asakusa:

Places we visited:

  • Asakusa
  • David Bull's Woodblock Shop
  • Tokyo Tower
  • Shibuya Crossing
  • Kamakura
  • Tokyo Disney
  • Tokyo DisneySea

Trip by the Numbers

A quick summary of our 16-day adventure:

Japan Trip — December 2024
City Days Main Highlights
Kanazawa 4 Samurai districts, Kenrokuen Garden, Shirakawa-go day trip
Kyoto 3 Golden Temple, Bamboo Forest, Nintendo Museum
Osaka 3 Super Nintendo World, Capcom Store, Godzilla Store
Nara 1 Deer Park
Tokyo 5 Asakusa, David Bull, Tokyo Tower, Disney parks
Total 16 A trip I'll never forget

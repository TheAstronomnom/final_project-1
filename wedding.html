Why Kennedy Space Center?

Living on Florida's Space Coast has always meant growing uparound rockets, launches, and the history of space exploration. Choosing the Kennedy Space Center as our wedding venue was Away to celebrate where we're from.

A few reasons it was the perfect venue:

  • Iconic location on Florida's Space Coast
  • Stunning views and unique backdrops
  • Meaningful to both of us as Florida locals
  • One-of-a-kind photo opportunities
  • A venue with a story
  • Celebrates Florida history
